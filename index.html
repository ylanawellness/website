About Ylana Wellness

Hi, I’m Ylana. Let’s make room for your well-being.

Holistic Health & Nutrition Coach | Fitness Instructor

I help women build the knowledge, strength, and everyday habits to care for themselves through the busy middle years of life.

My work is grounded in a simple belief: caring for your health should help you participate more fully in your life. It should give you more energy for your children, more confidence in your body, and a clearer sense of what works for you.

That’s why my approach brings nutrition, fitness, and mind-body healing together. How you eat, move, rest, and respond to stress all influence how you feel. We look at those connections while respecting your goals, preferences, and responsibilities.

Together, we’ll make sense of the health advice that feels overwhelming, explore the habits that are holding you back, and build practical skills you can use for years to come. You’ll learn to make food choices with greater confidence, move with purpose, and give your own needs a place in your day.

My coaching combines evidence-informed guidance with careful attention to your experience. Your body’s signals matter. So does a plan you can realistically follow.

I’m here to guide, encourage, and help you adjust as life changes—so your progress has room to last.

Services

Support that connects every part of your well-being.

Your goals may begin with weight loss, better energy, or feeling stronger. We’ll look at what supports those goals together and choose the right combination of nutrition, movement, and mind-body work for you.

Nutrition coaching

Feel confident about what—and how—you eat.

Move beyond the cycle of dieting, second-guessing, and starting over. Learn how to build satisfying meals, understand your nutritional needs, and make choices that support your energy, weight goals, and long-term health.

We’ll work with your family meals, schedule, and preferences so that eating well becomes a skill you can rely on—and model for your children.

1-on-1 personal training

Build strength you can feel in everyday life.

Feel more capable carrying your children, climbing stairs, and moving through a demanding day. Personalized training meets you at your current fitness level and helps you build strength, improve fitness, and grow more confident in your movement.

We’ll find a pace that challenges you appropriately and fits your capacity to recover, so consistency feels achievable.

Small group fitness

Make movement something you look forward to.

Build fitness alongside women who understand the demands of a full life. Small group sessions bring encouragement, connection, and shared momentum to your routine.

Through strength-focused movement and classes such as Zumba and Pilates, you can explore ways to feel stronger, more coordinated, and more at home in your body.

Mind-body healing

Create space to pause, reconnect, and restore.

When you spend much of your day responding to everyone else, it can be difficult to notice what you need.

Mind-body sessions offer space to explore the emotional patterns, stress, and expectations that shape how you care for yourself. Through guided reflection and grounding practices, we’ll support greater self-awareness and a deeper connection with your body, values, and spiritual well-being.

Wellness, inside & out

Thoughtful tools to support the work you’re doing.

The right products can make your daily routines simpler and more enjoyable. My wellness recommendations are an extension of our coaching: chosen with your needs, preferences, and goals in mind.

We’ll consider what each product offers, the evidence behind its intended use, and whether it adds something useful to your routine.

Everyday nourishment

Practical food and pantry recommendations that help you turn nutrition knowledge into satisfying meals—even on your busiest days.

Skincare with intention

Skincare options selected with attention to ingredients, your preferences, and the needs of your skin. A simple routine can become a small, consistent moment of care.

Targeted nutritional support

Supplements considered for a clear purpose, with attention to evidence, suitability, and any need for guidance from your healthcare professional. Natural ingredients deserve the same thoughtful consideration as any other product.

Movement and recovery essentials

Useful tools that help you bring movement, comfort, and restorative moments into your home routine.
